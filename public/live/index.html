What you are watching

One Yantrik OS machine, a sealed virtual machine working on its own. It holds no one's personal data.

What reaches it

Nothing from here. This page reads a video stream and has no way to send anything back. The relay takes one publisher on one path and serves the stream read-only, through this site's own server; everything else it could serve is switched off.

from deploy/live/relay/mediamtx.yml · nginx-live.conf

How it is walled off

Its video goes to a gate on a sealed network segment that has no path in from the LAN. The relay's secrets are kept on the gate, so nothing on the machine, its mind included, has them to show.

from publisher/publish · guest.sh · gate-forward/forward
